Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Agentic OS is a personal tool used only by its owner, on his own devices.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads his Google Tasks (the tasks.readonly scope): task titles, due dates, completion status and list names. It doesn't read task notes, and it can't create, change or delete tasks.

How the data is used

The tasks are shown on the owner's own dashboard, on his PC and his phone. Data moves only between those devices, over an encrypted private network.

Storage and sharing

  • The Google sign-in token stays on the owner's PC.
  • Tasks are held briefly in memory to show them, and the phone keeps its last copy for offline viewing.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.
  • Use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
